product

## Users

Postabl is built for indie hackers and X (Twitter) posters — solo builders and founders who share product shots, MRR charts, launch screenshots, and code captures on X and LinkedIn. Their context is fast and in-the-moment: they've just captured something in the middle of building or launching, and they want it to look good enough to post without breaking flow. The job to be done is turning a raw, flat screenshot into a framed, scroll-stopping image in seconds — from the browser, with no setup. The recurring surface is the editor; the marketing landing is the funnel into it.

## Product Purpose

Postabl turns raw screenshots into polished, postable images: upload a capture, frame it with padding, background, shadow, window chrome, and aspect ratio, then export as PNG, JPG, or WebP at up to 4×. It exists because the default screenshot — flat, cramped, edge-to-edge — is scroll-past material, and the existing ways to fix that are either fiddly design tools or lookalike screenshot apps. Success is measured by free-to-Pro conversion: free users (2 downloads/day, no account) reaching the value fast enough that upgrading to the $9.99/yr Pro plan — unlimited downloads, 4× exports, and the Brand Kit that stamps their handle and defaults on every export — becomes the obvious next step.

## Positioning

The fastest way to turn a raw screenshot into a scroll-stopping image. Every screen reinforces speed and zero friction: the shortest possible path from paste to export.

## Brand Personality

Witty, confident, and editorial. The voice is dry and self-assured with a little cheek — "Same pixels. Different posture.", "Small tool. Big difference.", "Stop posting ugly screenshots. You're better than that." It leans on serif display type and understatement rather than hype or exclamation. Opinionated but never loud; the confidence comes from taste, not volume.

## Anti-references

Not the generic SaaS-gradient look — purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates, and identical icon-card grids that read as default AI-SaaS. Not bloated or enterprise — heavy chrome, dense settings panels, forced onboarding, and "upgrade now" nags run directly against the zero-fluff promise.

## Design Principles

Zero fluff — every screen earns its place. No forced onboarding, no upgrade nags, no settings the task doesn't need. Paste, tweak, download.

Taste is the product. The value is opinionated defaults — backgrounds, padding, and shadows that look good immediately, so users get a great result without fiddling with pixel values.

Speed is a feature. The distance from raw capture to finished export stays short; friction anywhere on that path is a bug.

Anonymous-first. The tool works fully before anyone signs in. Capture signup intent at the moment of value — the Download, Save, or Pro action — not at the front door.

Show the difference. Prove the transformation (before/after, the exported result itself) rather than describing it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 contrast for body text, visible keyboard focus rings, and full reduced-motion support. Foundations are already in place — muted meta text was bumped to ~5.4:1 to pass AA, `:focus-visible` rings render for keyboard users, and `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the app's animations. Hold new work to the same bar.
